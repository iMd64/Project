Core Engineering PM Models & Formulas

Essential mental models and quantitative formulas for quick revision.

Model 01

The ICOM Conversion Model

Conceptualizes the project as an input-transformation-output system:

  • Inputs: Need or want encapsulated in a Project Brief.
  • Controls & Constraints: Time, cost, quality, legal, ethical, and environmental bounds.
  • Outputs: Tangible assets, information, or changed people (learning).
  • Mechanisms: People, tools, technology, and funds integrated by the PM.
Model 02

The 4-D Lifecycle Framework

Chronological progression connecting delivery with feedback:

  • D1 — Define: What & why? Produces the Project Brief.
  • D2 — Design: How, who, when? Produces the PID / Proposal.
  • D3 — Do (Deliver): Execute, monitor, and control. Produces Outcomes.
  • D4 — Develop: Learn & capture knowledge. Prevents Hedgehog Syndrome.
Quantitative 03

CPM Scheduling & Float Calculations

Key algorithms for 7-box Activity-on-Node (AoN) networks:

EFT = EST + Duration
LST = LFT - Duration
Total Float = LST - EST = LFT - EFT
Free Float = min(EST_successors) - EFT
Critical Path = Sequence where Total Float == 0
Diagnostic 04

The Project 7-S Diagnostic

Internal health check adapted by Maylor & Turner:

  • Strategy: High-level goals and means to achieve them.
  • Structure: Governance, decision owners, authority lines.
  • Systems: Planning, monitoring, quality, and change control procedures.
  • Staff & Skills: Headcount vs. specialized technical capability (e.g. PRINCE2).
  • Style & Stakeholders: Team culture and proactive communication plans.
