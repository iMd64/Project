Step 2: Forward Pass & Reverse Pass Step-by-Step Math

Slides 41–43 • How Every Single Number Inside the 10 Activity Boxes Is Calculated

6-Number Box Notation (Fig 6.10) Memorize This Box Layout Before the Quiz
• Top Row (Forward Pass → Left to Right): EFT = EST + Duration
  If a task has multiple predecessors: EST = MAX(all predecessor EFTs)
• Bottom Row (Reverse Pass ← Right to Left): LST = LFT − Duration
  If a task has multiple successors: LFT = MIN(all successor LSTs)
• Bottom Middle (Total Float): Float = LFT − EFT = LST − EST
1. Forward Pass (Left → Right) Slides 41–42
  • • Start Tasks (A, B, C start at Week 0):
    A: EST=0, Dur=4 ➔ EFT = 4
    B: EST=0, Dur=2 ➔ EFT = 2
    C: EST=0, Dur=6 ➔ EFT = 6
  • • Single Predecessor (D after A; E after B):
    D: EST=4, Dur=10 ➔ EFT = 14
    E: EST=2, Dur=2 ➔ EFT = 4
  • ★ Join 1 at F (preceded by C and E):
    EST(F) = max[EFT(C)=6, EFT(E)=4] = 6 (C controls F!)
    EFT(F) = 6 + 1 = 7
  • • Next Tasks (G after D; H after F):
    G: EST=14, Dur=3 ➔ EFT = 17
    H: EST=7, Dur=2 ➔ EFT = 9
  • ★ Join 2 at J (preceded by G and H):
    EST(J) = max[EFT(G)=17, EFT(H)=9] = 17 (G controls J!)
    EFT(J) = 17 + 20 = 37
  • • Terminal Task K (after G):
    K: EST=17, Dur=1 ➔ EFT = 18
    Project Finish: max[EFT(J)=37, EFT(K)=18] = 37 weeks
2. Reverse Pass (Right → Left) Slide 43
  • • Terminal Tasks (Both J & K get LFT = 37):
    J: LFT=37, LST = 37 − 20 = 17 ➔ Float = 0
    K: LFT=37, LST = 37 − 1 = 36 ➔ Float = 37 − 18 = 19
  • ★ Burst at G (G precedes both J and K):
    LFT(G) = min[LST(J)=17, LST(K)=36] = 17
    LST(G) = 17 − 3 = 14 ➔ Float = 0
  • • Publicity Branch (D and A):
    D: LFT=14, LST = 14 − 10 = 4 ➔ Float = 0
    A: LFT=4, LST = 4 − 4 = 0 ➔ Float = 0
  • • Teaching Branch (H and F):
    H (before J): LFT=17, LST = 17 − 2 = 15 ➔ Float = 15 − 7 = 8
    F (before H): LFT=15, LST = 15 − 1 = 14 ➔ Float = 14 − 6 = 8
  • • Syllabus & Staff Branches (C, E, B):
    C (before F): LFT=14, LST = 14 − 6 = 8 ➔ Float = 8 − 0 = 8
    E (before F): LFT=14, LST = 14 − 2 = 12 ➔ Float = 12 − 2 = 10
    B (before E): LFT=12, LST = 12 − 2 = 10 ➔ Float = 10 − 0 = 10

Possible Short-Answer Theory Questions on the Quiz

Slides 43–45 • Shared Float Trap, What Makes Week 37 Realistic? & "Garbage in, Gospel out"

Slide 43 • Shared Float Why Floats Cannot Be Added Together

Why can't you add C, F, and H's 8-week floats together?

⚠️ Exam Trap Answer On the teaching branch, C, F, and H each show 8 weeks of total float, and B and E each show 10 weeks of total float. However, these are total floats for individual tasks, NOT independent allowances to add together on one path. If Activity C is delayed by 8 weeks (finishing at week 14 instead of week 6), then F and H have 0 float left!
Slide 44 • Realism Check Beyond the Deterministic Network

What makes Week 37 realistic? (3 Factors)

1. Capacity: Can the same course staff prepare teaching material (J) while teaching other courses?
2. Calendar: Do holidays, application windows and room availability alter working weeks?
3. Uncertainty: Are publicity response, material approval and content changes represented?

5-Minute Exam-Day Action Checklist

Follow These 5 Steps in Order When You Receive the Quiz Sheet

Step 1 (1 min) Check the 10 activities (A–K, no I). Draw the 2 merges carefully: C & E → F, and G & H → J.
Step 2 (1 min) Write the 4 paths: ADGK=18, ADGJ=37 (Critical), BEFHJ=27, CFHJ=29.
Step 3 (1 min) Fill Top Rows (EST | Dur | EFT) left-to-right. Use max(6,4)=6 at F and max(17,9)=17 at J.
Step 4 (1 min) Fill Bottom Rows (LST | Float | LFT) right-to-left starting from 37 at both J and K. Use min(17,36)=17 at G.
Step 5 (1 min) Draw the Gantt Chart: list A..K down the left, mark weeks 0..37 across the bottom, and shade each bar from EST to EFT.
